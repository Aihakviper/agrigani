Diagnosis in three moves

Disease Library

Conditions we diagnose

Trained on Nigerian agricultural data. Covers crop diseases and livestock conditions, growing every planting season.

Maize Rust
Maize · Fungal

Orange-brown pustules on leaves. Spreads rapidly in humid conditions, causing severe yield loss.

Cassava Mosaic
Cassava · Viral

Whitefly-transmitted virus causing leaf distortion and stunted plant growth across entire plots.

Rice Blast
Rice · Fungal

Diamond-shaped lesions. Most destructive rice disease in West Africa. Strikes at any growth stage.

Tomato Late Blight
Tomato · Oomycete

Dark, spreading lesions on fruit and leaves. Entire harvests can collapse within 72 hours.
